In plain terms

This page tracks whether the City keeps talking about each goal year after year, or goes quiet on it. Going quiet doesn't prove the work stopped — but it's worth knowing which goals the City has and hasn't kept updating.

What a gap means

A missing annual update establishes only that the goal was not reported in that publication. It does not establish that implementation stopped, was abandoned, or was cancelled.
